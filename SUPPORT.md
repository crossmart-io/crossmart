# Getting help with Crossmart

- **Questions, ideas and "how do I…":** ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/crossmart-io/crossmart/discussions).
  Search first; someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/crossmart-io/crossmart/issues/new/choose) with
  the steps to reproduce, what you expected and what happened, and your Crossmart version.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** never in a public issue or discussion. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Crossmart Cloud customers:** use the in-app support in the dashboard. It knows your account.
- **Anything else:** hello@crossmart.io.

## What to expect

Crossmart is maintained by a small team. Self-hosted users are supported by the community and the
maintainers on a best-effort basis, with no guaranteed response times. Clear reports with
reproduction steps get answered first.
